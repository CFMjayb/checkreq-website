Quick guide

{{ guide.title }}

Year-end statements for donors: set the parish up once, make the list, build, and download. Beacon never sends one for you.

← Back to {{ guide.back_label }}

Where to find it

People & Giving → Statements (in the bar with People, Giving, Funds & campaigns and Pledges). It shows for people who hold the parish's Finance or Finance Supervisor role, once Giving is switched on for the parish. If you do not see it, you do not hold one of those roles here.

Set up once, before the first real run

1. The parish profile

Legal name, tax ID (EIN), address, the signer's name and title, a contact email, and whether to print the logo. Beacon fills the boxes from what it already holds (the parish record, the church office address, the clergy list), so check every one. The tax ID is printed on a tax document, so a person types or checks it. Press Save and confirm. Until the profile is confirmed you can still preview, but no real run can be made. Changing a confirmed profile takes the confirmation away until you confirm again.

2. The statement settings

A minimum year total (a donor under it gets no statement); whether to still build one for a donor with no mailing address (to hand out at church); a separate not tax deductible section; check numbers; and let members get their own statement online. Leave that last one off until you are ready.

3. The template

The template is the wording and look. The parish has one default. The first time a statement is needed, Beacon makes a copy of Cornerstone's Standard template for you. To change the wording, open Statement templates, copy a Cornerstone template to your parish, and edit your copy. Every save is a new version, and a preview with made-up data sits beside it. A copy never changes when Cornerstone changes its own; you are told when a newer version exists.

Look at one donor first

On the Statements page, search a donor's name (at least two letters) and press Preview. You see the statement as it would print for that year. A preview builds and stores nothing, and works before the profile is confirmed.

Run a year

  1. Make the list. On the Statements page choose Run statements. Pick the year, the template (the default unless you choose another), who (everyone, print only or email only), the order (name, ZIP code or envelope number), and the minimum. Typing a letter in Last name starts with makes a small test run. Press Make the list. Nothing is built yet.
  2. Read the list. It shows how many statements will be built and, for everyone left out, the plain reason, with a spreadsheet of the skipped. If you want to fix something (an address, a deposit still open), cancel the run, fix it, and make a new list: a year can have only one run open at a time.
  3. Build. Press Build the statements. Beacon builds a few at a time and shows progress. If you close the page, come back and press Build again: it carries on where it stopped. A statement that could not be built says why, and Try the failed ones again retries them. Cancel this run keeps everything already built.
  4. Download. All statements to print is one PDF for the donors who get a mailed copy, in the order of the list. The ZIP has a Print folder and an Email folder, one PDF per donor. Each statement also opens on its own. Every download is recorded.

What is on a statement

  • Only gifts in closed deposits count. A gift in a deposit that is still open is on no statement yet; the Run page tells you how many deposits are open.
  • Corrections are netted. A reversed, returned or moved gift is not listed twice: a gift moved to another fund shows once, under its final fund.
  • The year is the postmark date when a gift has one, otherwise the gift date, so a December-postmarked check deposited in January counts in December. The current year runs through today.
  • Tax-deductible gifts are listed by date and fund, with a total. When a donor received goods or services, their value reduces the deductible amount. Gifts in kind are listed by description with no dollar value; stock by symbol and shares. Gifts that are not tax deductible stay off unless you turned that section on. Staff notes never appear.
  • The name printed is first, middle, last and suffix, with no title. An organization prints its name.
  • A joint statement needs both spouses set to Joint on their records and linked as spouses. Otherwise each gets their own, and the list warns you.

Who is left out, with the reason shown: a person set to No statement; a donor with nothing to report; a total under the minimum; no mailing address for a printed copy (unless you allowed it); a donor marked do not mail; and placeholder records.

After a statement is built

Statements changed since issued (a link on the Run page) compares each built statement with the donor's gifts as they are now. A late gift, a correction or a reopened deposit makes a statement appear there. Re-issue builds a new, corrected statement. It says it is corrected and the date of the one it replaces. The old one is kept, marked replaced, and stays downloadable.

What members see

If you turn on let members get their own statement online (the profile must be confirmed and the parish's member screen must be on), a member sees Giving → Statements on their own page: each year with their gift count and tax-deductible total. Choosing a year builds that statement on the spot with the parish's default template. They see only their own giving, nothing is stored, each statement is logged, and a person can build ten an hour. A gift appears there once its deposit is closed, the same as on yours.

Good to know

  • Beacon never emails or mails a statement. It builds the files; your office prints or sends them.
  • A run is fixed to the template version it was made with, even if the template is changed afterwards.
  • Gifts are read again as each statement is built. Anything that changes later shows up under Changed since issued.
