Where to find it

On your parish's home page, choose HR & Payroll. You see it if you hold the Timekeeping, Parish Finance or Parish Admin role at your parish, and your diocese has turned it on for you. If you do not see it, ask your Parish Admin.

What is here

Staff

Everyone on your parish's staff list. Add a new person, correct a name or position, or tell the diocese someone has left.

Timekeeping

Enter the hours your staff worked in each pay period, then send them to the diocese.

The diocese reviews every change

You never change payroll yourself. Anything you do to your staff list is sent to the diocese's HR office, and it takes effect once they approve it. Until then you can take the change back. Beacon keeps names, positions and hours only: it never stores anyone's pay.
