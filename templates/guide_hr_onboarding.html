Follow each new hire and each departure until every step is done.

Where to find it

HR & Payroll → Onboarding & Offboarding. The board lists every open case, what is late and who it is waiting on. A red number on the tile counts the cases with something past due or a parish request waiting.

Working a case

Open a case to see its checklist. Each step is one box: what the step is, who owns it and when it is due, a note box, and three buttons on the right.

Done

The step is finished. A note is optional.

Waive

The step is not needed this time. A note saying why is required.

Not applicable

The step does not apply to this person. A note is required. Steps the checklist itself rules out are hidden: press Show not applicable items at the top to see them again. A step a person crossed out stays on the list, struck through, with their name and the date.

A finished step can be reopened. The buttons at the top open the person's details, the benefits coverage recorded so far, and the case history.

Setting up one person's checklist

Opening a new case takes you to the Set up the checklist screen, where you review this person's steps before work starts. It is also the Set up checklist button on the case page. Nothing here changes the standard checklist, only this person's copy.

Change a step

Reword it, move its due date or change who does it. The step shows who changed it.

Cross out a step

Use this when a step is not needed for this person. A short reason is required. It stays on the list, struck through, with your name and the date. Restore brings it back.

Add a step

For something this person needs that the standard checklist does not have.

Assign steps

Pick a person for any step and press Save assignments. They get a bell notice and see the step on My Steps. At EDOM and Claggett you can pick anyone with an episcopalmaryland.org, edom.org or claggettcenter.org login, plus anyone holding an HR role there. Other entities offer only their own logins.

The standard checklist can name a default person for each step (Checklist setup → Edit this checklist). That person is filled in when a case opens, if they can be assigned at that entity. A closed case cannot be changed.

If a step is assigned to you

Open My Steps from the menu under your name (it shows a number while steps are waiting). You see only the steps assigned to you: the person's name, the kind of case, the step and its dates. Press Done, with a note if you like. HR sees your name and the time on the step. You cannot see the rest of the case.

Filing documents

A step that expects a document (a signed form, an I-9, a letter) shows what is filed for it and an Upload File button. Press it, choose the file, and it is filed at once. The panel on the right lists everything filed on the case; press View to read a document there, or Download to save it. For a document that is not tied to a step, choose its kind at the top of that panel first, then press Upload File.

What can be uploaded

A PDF or a picture (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP), up to 25 MB. Save a Word document as a PDF first. A file is checked by what it really is, not by its name.

Where it goes

Every file is kept in two places in SharePoint: the entity's own HRPayroll folder and the central HR Administration folder. An I-9 is kept in one place only, in HR Administration. Beacon keeps no copy of the file itself.

Who can open it

HR Admins, Benefits Admins and Beacon Admins can open and file documents. An I-9 can only be filed and opened by an HR Admin. If you cannot open a document the list says so.

Replacing a document

Uploading another file for the same step does not delete the first. The new one becomes the current document and the old one stays under Earlier versions.

A document can still be filed on a finished case. If the upload says a folder is not set up yet, nothing was filed: ask the administrator to run the HR folder setup for the entity.

Requests from parishes

When a parish sends a new hire for benefits, it appears here as a case waiting on the diocese. Work it like any other case.

Everyone on the roster has a case

Whenever someone is added to the employee roster, Beacon opens an onboarding case for them: when you press Add employee on the Employees screen, when you approve a new hire in Roster Review, and when the payroll register shows someone new. The case is linked to their roster entry, and its note says where they came from.

If onboarding already happened elsewhere, open the case and close it as completed. A person found on the payroll register has no hire date on the case, because the register does not say when they were hired. Where staff are not filed by department, people on a church's own staff list (they work for the parish, not the diocese) do not get a case here.

Changing a checklist

On the board, press Checklist setup, pick a checklist and press Edit this checklist. Each step is one row. Press Edit on a row to change its wording, who does it, when it is due, whether it is required, the document it expects and who it applies to (everyone, clergy only, and so on). Add item, Remove and the arrows change the list on the page only. Nothing is saved until you press Save as new version.

A saved change is a new version. Cases already open keep the checklist they started with; new cases use the new one. Version history shows who changed it and when.

HR Admins change the staff checklists. Benefits Admins change the parish benefits checklists, which every diocese uses.

Closing a case

When every required step is done, choose Close as completed. Closing an onboarding case adds the person to the Employees roster. Closing an offboarding case marks them inactive as of their last day.
