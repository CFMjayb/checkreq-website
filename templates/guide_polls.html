Quick guide

{{ guide.title }}

Ask the people who hold a Beacon role a question by email, and see who has answered.

← Back to {{ guide.back_label }}

Where to find it

Administrative Tasks → Beacon → Polls & Surveys. You need to be a Beacon Admin at the entity you are working in. Polls belong to that entity: switch entities (top of the page) to see another entity's polls.

Make a poll in four steps

1. The message

A title, an optional subject and message, and which address it is sent from.

2. Who is asked

Add one or more groups. A group is a role (for example CFO, or Parish Clergy) at an entity. The page shows how many people that is, and who, as you choose. Someone in two groups is asked once. You can only pick entities where you are a Beacon Admin.

3. The questions

Yes / No, pick one, pick several, or a short written answer. One question makes a one-tap email. Two or more make a short survey with a single Respond now button.

4. Deadline and options

An optional deadline, optional automatic reminders (every so many days), whether people may change their answer, and a test run that sends every email to one address of yours.

Saving only keeps a draft. Nothing is sent until you open the poll and press Send now, after checking the list of people.

Sending

  • The list of people is frozen when you press Send. Roles granted or removed afterwards do not change who was asked.
  • Emails go out in batches while you watch. If you close the page halfway, open the poll again and it carries on without sending anyone twice.
  • Each person gets a private link. They do not need to sign in. With a single question, each choice is a button in the email: tapping it records the answer right away and shows the person what was recorded, with a Change my answer button (until the deadline, if you allowed changes). A survey has one Respond now button that opens the questions.
  • The tap is recorded by the page itself, not by merely opening the link, so email security scanners and link previews that just fetch the page do not answer for anyone. Every answer notes whether it came from the email button or the form.
  • Do a test run first when you are unsure: it is the same poll, but every email goes to one address and says who it would have gone to. Delete the test poll afterwards.

What the people you ask can see

  • Anyone who has a Beacon account and was sent a poll finds it again under My Polls in their name menu (top right). It lists the polls they were asked, what they answered, when, and anything still waiting for them, with an Answer now or Change my answer button while the poll is open. The menu item only appears once they have been sent a poll.
  • They see only their own answers. Nobody else's answers are ever shown to them, and test polls, drafts and cancelled polls do not appear.
  • Someone with no Beacon account only has the private link in their email.
  • While an administrator is impersonating someone, My Polls is view-only (no answering or changing for them) and shows only the entities that administrator is a Beacon Admin at.

Reading the results

  • Responded is the number to rely on: someone pressed Confirm.
  • Opened is only a hint. Many mail programs block or pre-load the tiny image it depends on.
  • Visited the link means the answer page was opened (by a person or a mail scanner). It is not an answer.
  • Someone who never answered by the deadline simply shows as Awaiting an answer. Beacon cannot see whether an email bounced; Email failed only means the email service refused it.
  • Each person's answers are listed under their name, and Download results gives you everything as a spreadsheet.

Reminders, closing and changes

  • Remind people who have not answered sends one more email to everyone still waiting (never to someone reminded in the last hour). Automatic reminders, if you set them, do the same on their own and stop at the deadline.
  • Close poll stops answers now. Cancel poll tells anyone who opens their link that it is no longer needed.
  • Remove next to a name takes one person off the poll (they cannot answer and are not reminded).
  • A draft can be edited or deleted. A poll that has been sent can be closed or cancelled but never erased, so there is always a record. Test polls can be deleted.
  • Everything that happens is kept in the Activity log at the bottom of the poll's page.
