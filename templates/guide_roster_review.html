Approve or reject the staff changes that parishes ask for.

← Back to {{ guide.back_label }}

Where to find it

HR & Payroll → Roster Review. A red number on the tile is how many changes are waiting. You need the HR Admin role at your diocese.

What each line is

One request from a parish: a new staff member, a correction, or someone leaving or returning. A new person who is already on the roster is flagged, with a Reactivate button when they are inactive.

Approve or reject

Approve

The change goes onto the staff list right away, and the parish is told.

Reject

You must give a reason. The parish sees it and can send a corrected request.

From Time Review

When you open a department in Time Review and it has requests waiting here, a warning at the top links to this screen, showing only that department.
