1. The message

What people see at the top of the page and in the email.

2. Who is asked

Add one or more groups. Each group is a role at an entity; a person who is in several groups is asked once. You can only choose entities where you are a Beacon Admin.

Choose a role to see who would be asked.

3. The questions

One question gives a one-tap email: yes/no or a short pick-one list can be answered straight from the email. Several questions make a short survey: the email has one Respond now button.

4. Deadline and options

After the deadline the link shows "closed" and no more answers are taken.

You can always send a reminder by hand from the poll's page.

Each email still carries a real person's private link, and its subject says who it would have gone to. A test poll can be deleted afterwards.

Cancel

Saving does not send anything. You review the list of people on the next page and press Send when ready.
