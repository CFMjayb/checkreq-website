1. What Is Parish Mode?

Parish Mode is a parish's own view of Beacon — separate from the diocese-wide Administrative side (Diocesan Mode) that staff who work across the whole diocese use. It's built for the people who work at a single parish: seeing the parish's own documents, announcements sent to it, its contact and leadership information, and (where it applies) its financial standing with the diocese — without needing access to anything belonging to any other parish.

2. Getting to Your Parish's Portal View

There are two ways someone ends up looking at a parish's Parish Mode view:

  • A parish's own staff or volunteers sign in directly with a login set up for that parish specifically. Their sign-in is already scoped to their own parish — there's no separate "switch into Parish Mode" step for them.
  • A diocesan staff member previews a parish from the Administrative Tasks screen's "Parish Mode" option, if they hold the right access — either a dedicated Parish Mode role, or one of the diocese's own administrative roles. This is the same idea as Beacon's separate "Impersonate a User" tool, but scoped to a parish instead of a person, and it's meant for support and troubleshooting, not day-to-day parish work.

3. What You Can Do There

What's actually available depends on the parish and on the specific access a person has been granted, but the common building blocks are:

Parish Documents

Files the diocese has shared with the parish (read-only), the parish's own working files, and a place to send documents to the diocese — organized by folder, browsable as a tree.

Announcements

A feed of dated notices the diocese has posted — either to every parish, or specifically targeted to this one.

Resource Library

A diocese-wide shared library of resources available to every parish.

Parish Information

The parish's own contact details, clergy, and lay leadership — kept current from the diocese's own records.

Parish Finance

Where it applies, a parish can see its Shared Ministry Allocation status by year, print a statement, see payments applied against it, and (for some parishes) a Middendorf loan balance — with a way to ask the Business Office a question or request an SMA direct-debit enrollment, right from the page.

Requests

A parish can send a general request or feedback to the diocese, and see the history of what it's already sent.

4. Access and Roles

A parish's own logins are managed separately from the diocese's — someone doesn't automatically get parish access just because they have a Beacon account elsewhere. A parish can have more than one person with access, each with their own role (for example, a parish administrator, a treasurer, or someone limited to documents only), and each role determines what that person can see and do for that one parish.

If you need access to your parish, or need to grant or remove someone else's, there's a "User Access" (or "Request Access") option available from inside your parish's own Parish Mode view.

5. Questions or Feedback

Beacon includes a built-in Feedback link in the navigation menu of every page — the fastest way to flag something confusing, report a problem, or suggest an improvement. For anything more urgent, or for questions this page doesn't answer, contact the CFM Business Office.
