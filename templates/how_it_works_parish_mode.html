1. What Is Beacon for Parishes?

Beacon for Parishes is part of a larger Diocesan tool called Beacon. In this section, Parish administrators, clergy and leadership have the opportunity to work with the Diocesan staff to share documents, announcements, the parish's contact and leadership information, and (where it applies) some limited financial standing with the diocese, in a safe and secure place.

3. What You Can Do There

What's actually available depends on the parish and on the specific access a person has been granted, but the common building blocks are:

Parish Documents

Files the diocese has shared with the parish and a place to send documents to the diocese.

Announcements

A feed of dated notices the Business Office has posted.

Resource Library

A diocese-wide shared library of resources available to every parish.

Parish Information

The parish's own contact details, clergy, and lay leadership — kept current from the diocese's own records.

Resources for Parishes

One tile on Parish Home that opens a short menu: the Neighborhood View, the Parish Sustainability Review, and the Resource Document Archive (shared diocese-wide documents).

Neighborhood View shows who lives within a 7-minute and a 15-minute drive of the church (ages, incomes, languages, housing, schools and more), from public Census data, with county, state and U.S. comparisons, and sets your parish's own numbers beside the neighborhood's. It appears once the diocesan office has confirmed the church's location.

Parish Sustainability Review shows ten years of your parochial report numbers and lets you work out what a priest would cost. It is shown to the people who see your parish's Finance section.

Parish Finance

Where it applies, a parish can see its Shared Ministry Allocation status by year, print a statement, see payments applied against it, and (for some parishes) a Middendorf loan balance — with a way to ask the Business Office a question or request an SMA direct-debit enrollment, right from the page.

4. Access and Roles

A parish's own logins are co-managed by the Business Office and each Parish's Administrator. A parish can have more than one person with access, each with their own role (for example, a parish administrator, a treasurer, or someone limited to documents only), and each role determines what that person can see and do for that one parish.

If you need access to your parish, or need to grant or remove someone else's, there's a "User Access" (or "Request Access") option available from inside your parish's own Parish Mode view.

5. Timekeeping: Reporting Hours to the Diocese

Your parish is set up for Timekeeping. Every two weeks the diocese runs payroll, and it needs the hours worked by your hourly staff. Timekeeping is where your parish reports them. Beacon only handles hours: it never shows or stores what anyone is paid. Staff who are paid a fixed salary are not on the list.

Who can use it: people who hold the Parish Finance or Parish Admin role for your parish. You will see an HR & Payroll tile on your parish's home page. Open it, then choose Timekeeping. The tile appears only when you have something to open there.

What happens each pay period

  1. The diocese opens the pay period. The Timekeeping page shows the period's dates and the Submit by date.
  2. You report the hours. Each hourly employee is one line. Type the total hours for the period for each kind (regular, overtime, holiday, sick, vacation), or open the person and enter the hours day by day. For one person and one kind of hours use a total or the days, not both. The totals add up as you type.
  3. Save as you go. Press Save as often as you like. Beacon asks before you leave the page with changes that are not saved.
  4. Submit the period. When the hours are right, press Submit Period to Diocese. That locks the hours for the period and sends along any employee-list changes you asked for. After that you cannot change them unless the diocese reopens the period.
  5. The diocese reviews them. The Business Office checks the hours, may correct them, and sends them on to payroll. Once a pay period is closed it cannot be changed.

If your parish sends hours by email instead

Some parishes email their hours to the diocese's payroll mailbox. If that is how your parish reports them, the diocese records them in Beacon for you, and you do not need to enter them again. Open Hours the diocese received to see exactly what was recorded for your parish, and whether each line is Recorded or still waiting for the diocese to review. If something looks wrong, tell the Business Office through a request.

Your employee list

The Staff Roster shows who is listed for your parish. To add a new hire, change someone's details or report that someone has left, use the Staff page under HR & Payroll. Nothing changes until the diocese approves the request, and it is sent with your next submission.

Good to know

  • Hours the diocese or an email recorded for a person show as locked on your screen. Ask the Business Office if one needs to change.
  • You always see the current period first. You can see what the diocese received for earlier ones under Hours the diocese received.
  • Questions about who is on the list, a missed deadline or a period that needs to be reopened go to the Business Office.
