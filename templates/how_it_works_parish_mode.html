1. What Is Beacon for Parishes?

Beacon for Parishes is part of a larger Diocesan tool called Beacon. In this section, Parish administrators, clergy and leadership have the opportunity to work with the Diocesan staff to share documents, announcements, the parish's contact and leadership information, and (where it applies) some limited financial standing with the diocese, in a safe and secure place.

3. What You Can Do There

What's actually available depends on the parish and on the specific access a person has been granted, but the common building blocks are:

Parish Documents

Files the diocese has shared with the parish and a place to send documents to the diocese.

Announcements

A feed of dated notices the Business Office has posted.

Resource Library

A diocese-wide shared library of resources available to every parish.

Parish Information

The parish's own contact details, clergy, and lay leadership — kept current from the diocese's own records.

Parish Finance

Where it applies, a parish can see its Shared Ministry Allocation status by year, print a statement, see payments applied against it, and (for some parishes) a Middendorf loan balance — with a way to ask the Business Office a question or request an SMA direct-debit enrollment, right from the page.

Requests

A parish can send a general request or feedback to the diocese, and see the history of what it's already sent.

4. Access and Roles

A parish's own logins are co-managed by the Business Office and each Parish's Administrator. A parish can have more than one person with access, each with their own role (for example, a parish administrator, a treasurer, or someone limited to documents only), and each role determines what that person can see and do for that one parish.

If you need access to your parish, or need to grant or remove someone else's, there's a "User Access" (or "Request Access") option available from inside your parish's own Parish Mode view.

5. Questions or Feedback

Beacon includes a built-in Feedback link in the navigation menu of every page — the fastest way to flag something confusing, report a problem, or suggest an improvement. For anything more urgent, or for questions this page doesn't answer, contact the Diocesan Business Office.
