How to read these numbers
  • Responded is the only number you can rely on: someone pressed the confirm button.
  • Opened is a best-effort signal. Outlook and Apple Mail often block or pre-load the tiny image it uses, so some people who read the email will not show, and some who did not may.
  • Visited the link means someone opened the answer page (or a mail scanner did). It is not an answer.
  • No answer means nothing was recorded by the deadline. Beacon cannot see whether an email bounced; "Email failed" only means the email service refused it.
