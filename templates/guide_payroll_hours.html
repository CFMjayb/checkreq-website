Parish hours that arrive by email, from the inbox to the Checkwriters sheet.

← Back to {{ guide.back_label }}

How it works

Each pay period, parishes email the hours of their hourly staff to the payroll mailbox. A routine reads that mail and records each person's period total in Beacon, marked Email and linked to the message it came from. Time Review is the checklist for the period. Staff key the hours into Checkwriters from the Excel sheet Beacon produces, or import them from the CSV file Beacon makes once the hours are Final (Checkwriters support has to set the import up first). Beacon holds hours only, never pay.

What the email reader does

In order, for each new email in the payroll mailbox:

  1. Reads it, and only reads it. The routine has read-only access to the mailbox. It never sends, replies, labels, moves or deletes anything.
  2. Decides the pay period. A staff pay-date label on the message wins. Otherwise the windows decide, and the noon deadline is soft: mail that arrives after it still belongs to that period until the pay date ends, unless the email says its hours are for the next pay period.
  3. Decides the parish. From the sender's address (the people listed on each parish's Profile), or from the parish named in a forwarded message. If it cannot tell, the email goes to review. It does not guess.
  4. Reads the content, including a timesheet in a photo, PDF, spreadsheet or Word attachment. The text of the email and those attachments is read by an AI (Claude) to pick out who worked how many hours. Attachments that may hold personal data (enrollment forms, W-4, I-9, direct deposit forms) are never sent to the AI, and Social Security numbers, bank numbers and birth dates are never copied.
  5. Matches each name to the parish's roster. A clear match is recorded. A close but uncertain match, or two people who fit, goes to review.
  6. Records the hours by type (regular, overtime, holiday, sick, vacation) as a period total with the source Email, the sentence the number came from, and a PDF copy of the email (see below).

Other mail is sorted, not recorded: a new hire or a termination becomes a pending request in Roster Review (the same queue the parishes use); a flat dollar amount such as a stipend is not hours, so it is listed for you to key by hand; a question or an automatic reply is set aside for a person.

What it never does

  • It never changes Checkwriters, Gmail or the paid register, and it never stores or shows pay.
  • It never overwrites hours you have confirmed. A different figure in a later email waits as a proposal for you to decide.
  • It never changes the roster by itself. New hires and terminations wait for approval.
  • It never records hours into a pay period whose hours are Final or that is closed, and it never records more than 300 hours for one person on one line. Mail like that is held for a person (see Late emails).
  • It handles each email once, so running it again does not double anything. Beacon applies the same rules whether the hours came from the routine, a parish or a person.

Where it stands

The reader is on for production. It runs about every 30 minutes on a diocesan computer (for now), so Beacon updates as the mail arrives. Everything it records is a line waiting for review: nothing counts as confirmed until a person accepts it. It is limited to the open pay period and the periods after it, and each email is handled once. If a day's mail looks wrong, check Time Review first: a red row means there are lines to look at, an amber row means that parish has sent nothing yet.

The Time Review screen

Open it from the HR & Payroll tile on the main menu. That menu also holds Roster Review and HR Administrative Tasks (Employees, Payroll Periods, Time Categories and HR Activation).

The top

The pill by the heading says whether the period is Open, Closed or Future (a Final pill appears once the hours are final). Change period lists every pay period so you can open another one. The blue Review Inbox button reads the payroll mailbox right now (it is available once the cloud reader is connected, and until then the button says so): a ring pulses around it while the review runs, and when it is finished the ring stops and Last updated changes. The Actions menu holds Set status (open or close the period), Excel for Checkwriters, CSV for Checkwriters import (available once the hours are Final: the same hours as a file Checkwriters can import, hours only and no pay; if something still needs a decision, or a kind of hours has no Checkwriters code, Beacon says so instead of making the file), Mark hours Final, Variance report and Carry forward standing hours. Only the parish rows scroll. At the bottom right of the screen, Last updated shows the date and time (MM/DD HH:MM) the payroll mailbox was last read; while a review is running it says so, and if the last one failed it says that too.

Status

The one pill in each row. Received by email means an email arrived for that parish. Hours recorded means hours are in Beacon. Ready to approve means hours are in and nothing is waiting. Approved, Confirmed no hours and Only Std Hrs (green) are done. Not Expected replaces Not Started for a parish set to No hours expected (set on its Profile) and is green. Changed after approval means hours or emails changed after the row was approved: look again and approve it again.

The other columns

Channel is how the parish gave its hours this period, Expected how it usually does, Emails how many arrived (hover for the time of the first). To review counts lines that still need a decision. Std is the standing hours (the same every period), Entered is every other hour (emailed, typed by the parish or by you), and Total is the two added together. Row colours: green means done (approved, confirmed no hours, confirmed standing hours, or Not Expected), blue is ready for you to approve, red has something waiting for a decision, amber is missing (a parish that normally sends hours every period has sent none yet), and a row with no colour has not started.

Approving a parish. In a row's three-dot menu: Mark reviewed and approved (when hours are in and nothing is waiting), Confirm no hours this week (a parish with no hours and no email), or Confirm standing hours (a parish with only standing hours). Beacon records who and when (hover the pill). Undo is in the same menu while the period is Open. A parish with standing hours and extra hours shows both, and one approval covers both.

A row's menu

The three dots open View / edit hours (see the lines under Period totals), Hours history (one row per pay period for that department) and Parish profile. Use Search and Show to find a parish or to see only the ones that need review, are missing hours, are ready to approve, are complete, have Only Std Hrs (only standing hours this period) or are not started.

People the emails named but nobody entered

If an email names someone the reader could not enter, that person is not lost. They come first in the Period totals area, under Named in the emails, not entered yet, beside the same email PDF as the people who were entered. Time Review counts them as lines to review, and the hours cannot be marked Final while any are left.

  • Why is shown for each: no exact hours in the email (only a limit such as “did not exceed 5 hours per week”), no one on the roster matches the name, more than one employee fits, or the reader was not sure.
  • Choose the employee from the list (the reader's best guess is already picked) and press Enter: the hours the reader read are entered for that person, tied to the same email and confirmed. When the email gave no exact figure, type the hours first.
  • Reactivate appears when the name is someone on the roster who is marked inactive. Pressing it (or approving the same request under Roster Review, or turning the person back on in Employees) makes them active again and enters the hours from that email for them. Those lines wait for your review like any other emailed hours. If the email gave no exact figure, nothing is entered and the name stays listed.
  • Dismiss when nothing is needed (for example you already entered the hours by hand). Who dismissed it and when are kept.
  • A name the reader had to match loosely (the same surname with a nickname such as Edie for Edith, or a surname read a little off) is entered but marked Name matched loosely: check it against the email. With two people of the same surname at a parish the first name has to match.

In the period totals each employee is one row, with Regular, Overtime and the other kinds of hours side by side.

Reviewing a line

Every line shows the hours, the source and the status. Point at Email (the Source) to see the sentence the number came from and what the email check found. If the email's PDF button is there, point at it for a preview of the email, or click it for a large view, so you can check what the reader understood.

  • One row per employee, with every kind of hours (Regular, Overtime, Holiday, Sick, Vacation) in its own box, like the summary grid. Change any figure right in the row, or type hours into an empty box to add that kind, then press Accept: it saves what you changed and confirms the row. Reject sets the whole row aside (it is kept, never deleted) and asks first.
  • Add a line (Actions menu at the top right of the Period totals area): for something the reader missed. One blank row opens at the top of the table; choose the employee, the kind of hours, the hours and, if you like, the email it came from (its PDF then shows with the line), then press Add. It is confirmed at once. Nothing takes up room until you ask for it.
  • Click a row in the Period totals and the daily grid below moves to that employee and flashes their line. (Clicking a box, a button or a link does what that control does.)
  • A different figure is waiting: a later email gave other hours for a line you already confirmed. The confirmed hours stay in force until you choose Use new figure or Keep current.
  • Conflict with the daily grid: the parish entered hours in Beacon and sent an email. Neither counts until you choose Use period total or Use daily grid.
  • New hire not on the roster: approve the new person in Roster Review first; their hours then move onto the roster row.
  • Accepted automatically: a NEW emailed line is confirmed the moment it arrives when the reader is at least 80% sure, the person is an hourly employee on the roster, nothing is flagged, and the hours are within 25% of that person's recent confirmed hours. The line says so, and the edit log says why. Everything else waits for you. A later email with different hours never replaces a confirmed line; it waits as a new figure. The 80% is a setting (payroll_auto_accept_confidence); 0 turns it off.
  • Accept all confirms the plain lines for a parish. It leaves flagged lines (much higher than usual, not an hourly employee, inactive) and anything above for you to look at.

Payroll notes (print or export)

Parishes write more than hours: a new position with new pay, a flat payment for a pianist, a note that someone is on normal hours, a question. The reader keeps every comment, note and request. Actions → Payroll notes on Time Review lists them all for the pay period, by department, each email with who sent it, when, its subject and its PDF. Print gives a clean paper copy; Export to Excel gives one row per note. Use it as the list of things to key or answer when you do the payroll. Personal data (Social Security, bank, birth dates) is never stored in a note.

Send reminder emails

While a pay period is Open, Actions → Send reminder emails on Time Review opens a screen of every department whose hours have not come in. It uses the same rule as the board: Missing hours (a department that normally sends every period or most periods and has sent nothing) and Not started. A department whose hours are in, or that is not expected to send, is left out.

  • Who gets it. Each row lists the people who emailed that department’s hours (Beacon learns them from the mail the reader sees, and they are already ticked), then the department’s payroll contacts (listed unticked). You can type one more address on the row. Tick Remember beside it to keep that address: once the email to the department has gone out, it becomes one of the department’s senders (added by hand) and is ticked from then on. Note that the payroll inbox reader also uses a department’s senders to tell which department an email belongs to, so remember only an address that belongs to that department alone. A department with no sender learned yet says so: tick a payroll contact or type an address.
  • Which departments. A tick box on each row. Missing hours departments that have a sender start ticked, Not started ones start unticked, and a department reminded in the last 24 hours starts unticked. Tick all with a sender and Untick all are above the list.
  • The message. Open Message to read or change it. {department} becomes each department’s name. Leave square brackets out of the subject: the mail system drops mail that has them. Tick Save this wording as the standard message and send: from then on every reminder starts from your wording, for the whole diocese. This pay period’s dates are kept as {period} and {deadline}, so the saved message is right for every later pay period. Use the original wording forgets it.
  • Sending. Send reminders asks first, then sends one email per ticked department to its ticked addresses. The email comes from the Beacon notification address and answers go to the payroll mailbox, so a reply lands where the reader looks. If one email fails the rest still go; the row says what went wrong. Last reminded shows when each department was last sent a reminder.
  • Nothing is sent by accident. Beacon sends only to addresses it offered for that department or one you typed, and checks everything before it sends anything: a mistake means nothing is sent and your ticks are kept. On the development site every email goes to the test address, never to a department.

Late emails

Once a pay period's hours are marked Final, the reader stops recording hours from email for it. An email with hours that arrives after Final or after the period is closed, or that the reader cannot place (no church it can tell, or no pay period that fits), is held instead. Actions → Late emails on Time Review lists each one with who sent it, when, its PDF, and the people and hours the reader found. A red number on the Time Review tile and a red banner on the board show how many are waiting. Nothing is recorded until a person presses a button.

  • Reopen and record. The pay period is no longer Final (a closed one is reopened), the hours are recorded waiting for review, and the period has to be marked Final again.
  • Carry to next period. The same, into the next pay period.
  • Pay by manual check. The hours are recorded in the pay period they belong to, with a note, the same way the tie-out treats a manual check. The paid register will not have them, so the Variance report shows the difference: mark it Manual check there. Nothing is decided on this screen.
  • Dismiss as not hours. Nothing is recorded. The email stays in the Decided list.
  • Pick the church for an email whose church could not be told. Then the buttons above work.
  • Repeats. Beacon checks every line against hours already entered (and not rejected) for that person and kind of hours. A line that matches is marked already entered; a different figure shows what Beacon already has. An email whose every line is already entered is tagged Looks like a repeat and offers Dismiss as already entered; Dismiss all repeats does every such email at once. The check runs again when you press a button, an already-entered line is never entered twice, and nothing dismisses itself.

Mark hours Final is refused while anything waits in this list. When mail arrives after Final, Beacon sends one email per reader run to the diocese's accounting address (for Maine, accounting@episcopalmaine.org) naming the department, the sender and the subject, with no hours in it. Only emails with hours are held: questions, administrative mail and funding-report replies are set aside as before.

The Timekeeping screen (View / edit hours)

The same screen the parish uses, opened on the parish's behalf. The title row, the period and the grand total stay at the top and the bottom, with a gold Save at each end. Only the people scroll.

  • Period totals (diocese view only) is its own scrolling area at the top: one row per employee with every kind of hours editable (Reg, OT, Sick, Vac, Holiday, in the same order and the same columns as the daily grid below), one Accept and one Reject for the row, and Actions > Add a line. Drag the bar between the two grids up or down to give one more room (it remembers where you left it; double-click puts it back).
  • Each person starts as one line with a box per category: type that person's total for the period. The note says Expand to enter by day. The arrow opens the days.
  • By day: the dates (with the day of the week) are shown inside each person's opened area, not in the heading. Narrow boxes take only numbers like 7.50 (up to 24 a day); numbers are right-justified and line up down the page. The category column on the left and the total on the right stay in place. The foot of each person's block is their total, and the grand total at the top and bottom changes as you type.
  • A total or the days, not both, for the same person and category. While a total is typed the days are locked; while any day is typed the total is locked and shows the days' sum. Clear one to use the other. A total typed over an email's figure replaces it as the diocese's figure; clearing a total takes it off (it is kept, never deleted).
  • Per-type totals on the person's line (in the Reg, OT, Sick, Vac and Holiday columns, under the same headings as the Period totals above) follow what is typed, whether the days are open or not. A total box is locked while days are entered. Clear days, keep totals (on the open line) empties the day boxes and keeps each type's total, now editable. Right-click a type's name to do it for just that type.
  • Only what you changed is saved, and Beacon says how many changes it saved and why it refused any. It asks before you leave with changes unsaved.
  • The email's PDF: a masked copy of the email the reader recorded the hours from, made for review. Social Security, bank and birth-date numbers are blacked out; forms that may hold personal data are listed but never included. Only HR Admins can open it.

A parish sees the same screen, with a Submit Period to Diocese button. It can type its own totals (they wait for your review) and take back one it entered, but it cannot change a total you or an email recorded.

Standing hours

For people whose hours are the same every period (type the hours in the Std hrs column of the Employees screen, under HR & Payroll > HR Administrative Tasks; clear the box to stop. The person must report hours and have an employee number), Actions > Confirm all standing hours (or Confirm standing hours in one parish's menu) records them for the period as Regular hours. They are confirmed automatically when the last three periods were confirmed at the same figure; otherwise they wait for review. A parish that has only standing hours turns green (Only Std Hrs); a parish that also has other hours still needs its approval. If an email later gives different hours, it appears as a waiting figure.

Hours history

A row for every pay period, newest first, with the hours counted for each kind of hours (Reg, OT, Sick, Vac, Holiday), the Total, the Paid figure once the paid hours are loaded, and how many lines are still Waiting for review (a waiting line is not counted yet). Open it for one employee from the three-dot menu on the Employees screen, or for one department from its three-dot menu on Time Review; each page links to the other, and a period's name opens that period for the department. Pay #1 to #20 were loaded from the paid labor distribution, so for those periods Beacon's hours are the paid hours. It shows hours only, never pay.

Final, the Excel sheet and the variance report

  • Excel for Checkwriters (in the Actions menu) always works. Its first sheet, Summary, has one row per employee with Reg, OT, Sick, Vac and Holiday, then Entered total, Standing and Grand total, a subtotal after each department and a grand total at the bottom, so each department can be compared with the Labor Summary. The Review sheet says whether the hours are FINAL and lists everything still unresolved. Do not key a line that is listed there.
  • Mark hours Final (in the Actions menu) needs nothing waiting for a decision, every parish row green and nothing waiting in Late emails. It tells you how many are not. A figure a person types afterwards takes the stamp away; an email can no longer change it (it is held in Late emails instead).
  • Variance report, after payroll runs: Beacon's hours against the paid register, for employees who report hours. It lists matches, differences, people paid with no Beacon hours and Beacon hours with no pay, with the Std and Entered hours Beacon counted.

Closing out a pay period

After payroll is processed, open Time Review > Actions > Close out this pay period. It lists the steps, what is done and what is waiting. Every step is safe to run again.

  1. Hours are Final. Approve every department, then Mark hours Final.
  2. Close the pay period. Hours stop landing in it.
  3. Load the paid hours. On the payroll PC, double-click Close Out Pay Period.bat. It reads the labor distribution in the pay date's folder, checks it against the Checkwriters register, and sends hours only to Beacon. Pay never leaves the PC.
  4. Tie out. Beacon's hours against the paid hours, with the departments that differ listed. Every line that differs gets one decision, line by line or for a whole person (see below).
  5. Actualize. It waits until every differing line has a decision, then one button applies them. The old figure stays in the edit history, a person paid with no Beacon hours gets a line, and Beacon hours that were not paid become 0 with a note. A person whose total hours across all categories match the paid total is left as entered (for example Holiday or Sick hours that payroll paid as Regular): nothing is changed, a note for review is added to each of their lines, no decision is needed, and the Variance report shows them as “Total matches, categories differ”.
  6. File the reports. Either the Phoenix workbook's File Reports to Beacon Documents button has already posted each department's two reports (press Reports Posted via Phoenix), or press File Reports Now Here and Beacon copies this pay date's department reports from the Accounting library (the year's Payroll Reports, then the pay date's folder) into each department's HRPayroll > Payroll Reports > PD… folder on the Documents site. It copies only, never overwrites a file that differs, never files the all-department reports, and says what it did. Beacon records who and when (Undo takes it back). It needs the DME administrator to have given Beacon read access to the Accounting site once; until then it says so and files nothing.
  7. Open the next pay period. One button, available once this period is closed.

Settling a difference

On the Variance page (See each difference), each line that differs has a Decision pick list, and each person's lines are drawn together in one box. A person with several differing lines also has a pick list under their name that sets them all at once: it replaces whatever decision those lines already had (a line the choice cannot fit keeps its decision, and the message says which). Once a decision is saved, an Undo button appears beside the pick list and takes it back, and a note box (a check number, a date) appears.

  • Include in next period. Beacon has more hours than were paid and the extra hours will be paid in the next pay period. This period takes the paid hours. The difference is added to that person's line in the same category of the next period, marked “Includes hours carried in”, so it is in that period's hours to key. An emailed figure that arrives later is added on top of the carried hours. A figure someone types is taken as typed.
  • Manual check / void-reissue. The difference was settled outside the regular payroll run. Either Beacon has more hours than were paid because they were already paid by an interpay manual check, or Beacon has fewer hours than were paid, Beacon was right, and the over-paid check was voided and reissued. This period keeps its hours as entered, with a note. Nothing is added to the next period and nothing is keyed. The next period's register is expected to show the amount (a plus for a manual check, a negative for a void and reissue) and its tie-out does not count it as a difference. If it is not on that register, the next period's close-out page lists it as “Expected on this register, not found”.
  • Beacon wrong. The paid hours are right. Beacon takes them (this is what Actualize always did).

Include in next period fits only a line where Beacon has more hours than were paid. Beacon wrong for all N remaining (bottom of the Variance page) decides every line that has none yet. If the paid hours are loaded again and a line's difference changes, its decision shows “The paid hours changed since this was decided” and has to be made again. Once Actualize has applied the decisions they are locked, and every change is in the edit history. The next period's close-out page has a From the previous pay period box that lists the hours to key and the manual-check and void-reissue amounts that must not be keyed.

On the Checkwriters Excel, manual-check and void-reissue amounts have their own column, Manual check (do not key). They are never part of the hours to key. They are only added into a separate Total hours column, so each department can be compared with the Labor Summary.

A parish's profile

The Parish profile item in each row's menu sets how the parish usually gives its hours and lists the people who send them. New senders are added as mail arrives; you can add or remove one here. They are kept apart from the people who receive the payroll reports.

What a parish sees

A parish's Timekeeping page has an Hours the diocese received link: a read-only list of what was recorded from its emails, so the parish can see it was understood.
