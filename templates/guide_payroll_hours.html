Parish hours that arrive by email, from the inbox to the Checkwriters sheet.

How it works

Each pay period, parishes email the hours of their hourly staff to the payroll mailbox. A routine reads that mail and records each person's period total in Beacon, marked Email and linked to the message it came from. Time Status is the checklist for the period. Staff key the hours into Checkwriters from the Excel sheet Beacon produces. Beacon holds hours only, never pay.

What the email reader does

In order, for each new email in the payroll mailbox:

  1. Reads it, and only reads it. The routine has read-only access to the mailbox. It never sends, replies, labels, moves or deletes anything.
  2. Decides the pay period. A staff pay-date label on the message wins. Otherwise the deadline windows decide: mail up to noon on a period's submission deadline belongs to that period.
  3. Decides the parish. From the sender's address (the people listed on each parish's Profile), or from the parish named in a forwarded message. If it cannot tell, the email goes to review. It does not guess.
  4. Reads the content, including a timesheet in a photo, PDF, spreadsheet or Word attachment. The text of the email and those attachments is read by an AI (Claude) to pick out who worked how many hours. Attachments that may hold personal data (enrollment forms, W-4, I-9, direct deposit forms) are never sent to the AI, and Social Security numbers, bank numbers and birth dates are never copied.
  5. Matches each name to the parish's roster. A clear match is recorded. A close but uncertain match, or two people who fit, goes to review.
  6. Records the hours by type (regular, overtime, holiday, sick, vacation) as a period total with the source Email, the sentence the number came from, and a link to the email.

Other mail is sorted, not recorded: a new hire or a termination becomes a pending request in Time Review (the same queue the parishes use); a flat dollar amount such as a stipend is not hours, so it is listed for you to key by hand; a question or an automatic reply is set aside for a person.

What it never does

  • It never changes Checkwriters, Gmail or the paid register, and it never stores or shows pay.
  • It never overwrites hours you have confirmed. A different figure in a later email waits as a proposal for you to decide.
  • It never changes the roster by itself. New hires and terminations wait for approval.
  • It never records hours into a closed pay period, and it never records more than 300 hours for one person on one line.
  • It handles each email once, so running it again does not double anything. Beacon applies the same rules whether the hours came from the routine, a parish or a person.

Where it stands

The reader is on for production. It runs about every 30 minutes on a diocesan computer (for now), so Beacon updates as the mail arrives. Everything it records is a line waiting for review: nothing counts as confirmed until a person accepts it. It is limited to the open pay period and the periods after it, and each email is handled once. If a day's mail looks wrong, check Time Status first: a red row means there are lines to look at, an amber row means that parish has sent nothing yet.

The Time Status screen

The top

The pill by the heading says whether the period is Open, Closed or Future (a Final pill appears once the hours are final). Change period lists every pay period so you can open another one. The Actions menu holds Set status (open or close the period), Excel for Checkwriters, Mark hours Final, Variance report and Carry forward standing hours. Only the parish rows scroll.

Status

The one pill in each row. Received by email means an email arrived for that parish. Hours recorded means hours are in Beacon. Not Expected replaces Not Started for a parish whose pattern is standing hours or none (set on its Profile).

The other columns

Channel is how the parish gave its hours this period, Expected how it usually does, Emails how many arrived (hover for the time of the first). To review counts lines that still need a decision. Row colours show what is left: green is complete, red has lines waiting for a decision, amber is missing (a parish that normally sends hours every period has sent none yet), grey is not expected.

A row's menu

The three dots open View / edit hours (see the lines under Period totals) and Parish profile. Use Search and Show to find a parish or to see only the ones that need review, are missing hours or are complete.

Reviewing a line

Every line shows the hours, the source, the sentence the number came from and a link to the email, so you can check what the reader understood.

  • Accept confirms it. Save hours corrects it and confirms it. Reject sets it aside (it is kept, never deleted).
  • A different figure is waiting: a later email gave other hours for a line you already confirmed. The confirmed hours stay in force until you choose Use new figure or Keep current.
  • Conflict with the daily grid: the parish entered hours in Beacon and sent an email. Neither counts until you choose Use period total or Use daily grid.
  • New hire not on the roster: approve the new person in Time Review first; their hours then move onto the roster row.
  • Accept all confirms the plain lines for a parish. It leaves flagged lines (much higher than usual, not an hourly employee, inactive) and anything above for you to look at.

Standing hours

For people whose hours are the same every period (set Hours basis: standing and the hours on the Employees screen), Carry forward standing hours records them for the period. They are confirmed automatically when the last three periods were confirmed at the same figure; otherwise they wait for review. If an email later gives different hours, it appears as a waiting figure.

Final, the Excel sheet and the variance report

  • Excel for Checkwriters (in the Actions menu) always works. Its first sheet, Review, says whether the hours are FINAL and lists everything still unresolved. Do not key a line that is listed there.
  • Mark hours Final (in the Actions menu) is offered only when nothing needs a decision. A new figure arriving afterwards takes the stamp away.
  • Variance report, after payroll runs: Beacon's hours against the paid register, for employees who report hours. It lists matches, differences, people paid with no Beacon hours and Beacon hours with no pay.

A parish's profile

The Parish profile item in each row's menu sets how the parish usually gives its hours and lists the people who send them. New senders are added as mail arrives; you can add or remove one here. They are kept apart from the people who receive the payroll reports.

What a parish sees

A parish's Timekeeping page has an Hours the diocese received link: a read-only list of what was recorded from its emails, so the parish can see it was understood.
