How it works

Each pay period, parishes email the hours of their hourly staff to the payroll mailbox. The payroll inbox routine reads the mail and records each person's period total in Beacon, marked with where it came from and a link to the email. Time Status is the checklist for the period. Nothing here ever holds pay: hours only.

The Time Status board

Status

Received by email means an email arrived for that parish. Not Expected replaces Not Started for a parish whose pattern is standing hours or none (set on its Profile).

Channel, Expected, Emails

How the parish gave its hours this period, how it usually does, and how many emails arrived (with the time of the first).

To review

Lines that still need a decision from you. Open the parish (View / Edit) to see them under Period totals.

Reviewing a line

Every line shows the hours, the source, the sentence the number came from and a link to the email.

  • Accept confirms it. Save hours corrects it and confirms it. Reject sets it aside (it is kept, never deleted).
  • A different figure is waiting: a later email gave other hours for a line you already confirmed. The confirmed hours stay in force until you choose Use new figure or Keep current.
  • Conflict with the daily grid: the parish entered hours in Beacon and sent an email. Neither counts until you choose Use period total or Use daily grid.
  • New hire not on the roster: approve the new person in Time Review first; their hours then move onto the roster row.
  • Accept all confirms the plain lines for a parish. It leaves flagged lines (much higher than usual, not an hourly employee, inactive) and anything above for you to look at.

Standing hours

For people whose hours are the same every period (set Hours basis: standing and the hours on the Employees screen), Carry forward standing hours records them for the period. They are confirmed automatically when the last three periods were confirmed at the same figure; otherwise they wait for review. If an email later gives different hours, it appears as a waiting figure.

Final, the Excel sheet and the variance report

  • Download Excel for Checkwriters always works. Its first sheet, Review, says whether the hours are FINAL and lists everything still unresolved. Do not key a line that is listed there.
  • Mark hours Final is allowed only when nothing needs a decision. A new figure arriving afterwards takes the stamp away.
  • Variance report, after payroll runs: Beacon's hours against the paid register, for employees who report hours. It lists matches, differences, people paid with no Beacon hours and Beacon hours with no pay.

A parish's profile

The Profile button on each parish row sets how it usually gives its hours and lists the people who send them. New senders are added as mail arrives; you can add or remove one here. They are kept apart from the people who receive the payroll reports.

What a parish sees

A parish's Timekeeping page has an Hours the diocese received link: a read-only list of what was recorded from its emails, so the parish can see it was understood.
