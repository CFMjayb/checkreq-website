Non-contribution accounts

Money a parish takes in that is not a gift (rent, a reimbursement, event tickets, money passed through to someone else) goes to a GL account, not to a fund. These are the accounts this parish allows for it. A line of type “Non-gift receipt” takes the default account unless the clerk picks another, and it cannot be saved or closed without one. These receipts never appear on giving statements or tax receipts and never count toward pledges or giving totals.
