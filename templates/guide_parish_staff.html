Where to find it

HR & Payroll → Staff. Each person is one line. Use the search box to find someone, and Show to see only active people, only inactive people, or only people with a change waiting.

Onboard a new staff member

  1. Choose + Onboard a new staff member at the top right.
  2. Fill in the name, the position and, if you know them, the employee number and hire date. Choose Submit.
  3. The diocese reviews the new person before they join your list. Until then they show on your list as New staff member waiting.
  4. If your diocese uses onboarding, a button offers Continue to onboarding. That sends the hire to the diocese's benefits office so health benefits can be set up. You can skip it, and do it later from the person's line.

Edit and Status change

Edit

Fix a name, position or employee number. The diocese approves the correction before your list changes.

Status change

Use this when someone leaves, or comes back. Choose what the change is (for example Voluntary resignation or Termination), enter the last day of work, and choose Review. Read the sentence, then Confirm.

The person stays on your list as they are until the diocese approves.

Taking a change back

A person with a change waiting shows a Withdraw button instead of Edit and Status change. Withdraw it and nothing goes to the diocese. Once the diocese has answered, it can no longer be withdrawn: send a new change instead. Proposed changes at the top lists everything you have sent and what came of it.
